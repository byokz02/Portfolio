01 / About me

Code, curiosity & a bit of everything.

I'm Kervie Compra Roca, a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student based in Cebu City. I like building things people can actually click on: web apps, small games, and tools that make a messy task simpler.

Most of my projects start as team builds. A Laravel racing game with a live leaderboard, a Django lost-and-found system for school, a card game with my teammates. Along the way I've picked up graphic design and hands-on hardware work: assembling, configuring and troubleshooting PCs.

My approach is simple. Break the problem down, ship something that works, then make it better. I'm adaptable, comfortable juggling several tasks at once, and happy to lead or to pitch in, whatever the team needs.
